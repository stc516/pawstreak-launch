Pet-friendly hotels that make bringing the dog easier.

“Pet friendly” can mean anything from “we tolerate one small dog for a fee” to actual bowls, beds, treats and dog-walking resources. These San Diego options stand out because the pet program is part of the stay—not an afterthought.

Before booking: Pet fees, weight limits and room rules change. Confirm the current policy directly with the hotel before making a special trip.
DOWNTOWN

Hotel Indigo San Diego

San Diego tourism highlights a dedicated pet program with bowls, loaner beds and treats, historically with broad flexibility on pet size and number. A particularly convenient base if you want walkable downtown access.

DOWNTOWN

Manchester Grand Hyatt San Diego

A waterfront downtown option that welcomes cats and dogs subject to its current weight and fee policy, with pet amenities available during the stay.

DOWNTOWN

The Sofia Hotel

A centrally located option with designated pet-friendly accommodations. Confirm nightly fees and room availability when booking.

DOWNTOWN

Hotel Republic San Diego

A dog-forward downtown stay that has offered pet welcome extras and outdoor dining perks—useful if your trip is built around walkable city exploring.

LA JOLLA / TORREY PINES

Hilton La Jolla Torrey Pines

A good fit for people who want coastal North County access and proximity to Del Mar. Tourism guidance lists dogs under the hotel’s current size/fee rules.

CORONADO

Loews Coronado Bay Resort

One of the more developed pet programs in the region: walking-route information, bowls, bedding and other pet resources have all been part of the Loews Loves Pets program. Resort restrictions still apply in restaurants, fitness areas and pool decks.

How we’ll score hotels later

PawStreak Places will eventually distinguish between pets technically allowed and a genuinely easy dog stay using factors such as fees, size limits, grassy relief areas, walkability, dog amenities, nearby parks and whether dogs can comfortably join you around the property.
