# Bailey & Omi Adventures — Books 2–11 editorial roadmap

The creative bible and Book 1 are authoritative. These are development concepts, not approved manuscripts or illustration briefs.

1. **Book 2 — Bailey & Omi and the Windy-Day Chase** (recommended next)

Book 2 is first because it is active, funny, visually distinct from an indoor staircase, easy to ground in a real Southern California outing, and gives Bailey useful discoveries without making Omi infallible.

## Book 2 — Bailey & Omi and the Windy-Day Chase

- **Core adventure / setting:** A family outing at a dog-friendly coastal park turns lively when a gust scatters a few safe picnic items across grass, shrubs, and a low footbridge.
- **Bailey’s role:** Her curiosity notices how the wind moves objects and helps predict where the last lightweight item went.
- **Omi’s role:** She confidently chases the most obvious object, then has to reconsider when speed is not enough.
- **Relationship dynamic:** Bailey discovers the pattern; Omi supplies persistence. Each solves a different part.
- **Conflict:** They follow different clues and briefly work at cross-purposes while their grown-up keeps the adventure safe.
- **Funny moments:** Omi repeatedly corners the wrong fluttering leaf; Bailey’s fluff blows into a comically grand silhouette; an item lands almost where they started.
- **Emotional arc:** Excitement becomes frustration, then attentive teamwork and shared delight.
- **Resolution:** Bailey uses observation, Omi uses a careful route, and the sisters reunite the last item with the picnic.
- **Underlying theme:** Curiosity and experience work better together than either one alone.
- **Visual opportunities:** Coastal light, eucalyptus shapes, flying napkin/hat/map shapes, windswept fur, readable chase paths.
- **Difference from Book 1:** Outdoor comedy and cooperation replace fear-and-courage progression; Bailey contributes the key insight.
- **Difference from later books:** Air/motion and playful retrieval, not water, navigation, or nighttime uncertainty.

## Book 3 — Omi’s Unexpected Splash

- **Core adventure / setting:** At a calm, dog-safe shoreline or splash-friendly creek edge, Omi assumes she understands the water while Bailey experiments cautiously from shore.
- **Bailey’s role:** Notices shallow stepping spots and shows Omi a calmer way across.
- **Omi’s role:** Begins overconfident, gets surprised by a harmless splash, then accepts Bailey’s lead.
- **Relationship dynamic:** Bailey helps Omi without becoming suddenly fearless or expert at everything.
- **Conflict:** Omi’s preferred direct route is wetter and less comfortable than expected.
- **Funny moments:** A reflection startles Omi; Bailey tests puddles with one exaggerated paw; both shake water at the same time.
- **Emotional arc:** Omi’s confidence wobbles; Bailey’s careful observation becomes reassurance; both regain ease.
- **Resolution:** They choose a shallower shared route and enjoy the water on their own terms.
- **Underlying theme:** Experience includes knowing when to change your mind.
- **Visual opportunities:** Reflections, ripples, wet/dry fur texture, bright shore colors, mirrored poses.
- **Difference from Book 1:** The older sister faces uncertainty and Bailey helps.
- **Difference from other future books:** Physical sensory surprise rather than wind, route choice, or darkness.

## Book 4 — Bailey & Omi and the Two-Trail Tangle

- **Core adventure / setting:** A signed, dog-friendly canyon loop presents two safe trails with different sights before rejoining.
- **Bailey’s role:** Wants the lively trail full of scents and small discoveries.
- **Omi’s role:** Prefers the familiar, quieter-looking route.
- **Relationship dynamic:** They disagree without one being wrong; separate clues from both routes explain the whole place.
- **Conflict:** Their attention pulls in opposite directions, making progress awkward until they learn to alternate choices.
- **Funny moments:** Both step toward different signs at once; an interesting scent seems to “move” because the breeze crosses trails; they meet the same landmark from opposite angles.
- **Emotional arc:** Impatience becomes listening, compromise, and appreciation for different ways of exploring.
- **Resolution:** They choose one route out and the other back, each leading for part of the loop.
- **Underlying theme:** A shared adventure can hold more than one good choice.
- **Visual opportunities:** Split compositions, canyon golds, trail signs, overhead route shapes, changing leader positions.
- **Difference from Book 1:** The challenge is disagreement and compromise, not fear.
- **Difference from other future books:** Built around choice and perspective rather than an external object or surprising environment.

## Book 5 — Bailey & Omi Under the Stars

- **Core adventure / setting:** A supervised evening backyard camp or dog-friendly campsite in Southern California introduces night sounds, shadows, and a sky they rarely pause to notice.
- **Bailey’s role:** Eagerly investigates a sound and invents increasingly silly explanations.
- **Omi’s role:** Tries to appear unbothered but misreads a shadow herself.
- **Relationship dynamic:** Both misunderstand different clues; together they pair sound, scent, and sight with ordinary nighttime sources.
- **Conflict:** Their imaginations make harmless surroundings feel stranger than they are.
- **Funny moments:** A “giant creature” is their own doubled shadow; leaves sound like footsteps; both jump at a soft tent flap.
- **Emotional arc:** Playful bravado and uncertainty settle into wonder and closeness.
- **Resolution:** The last mystery becomes a familiar night bird or wind in leaves, and the sisters rest beneath the stars.
- **Underlying theme:** Wonder grows when you stay curious together.
- **Visual opportunities:** Indigo watercolor, warm lantern pools, expressive silhouettes, star fields, sisterly resting pose.
- **Difference from Book 1:** Both dogs are uncertain and jointly investigate; the goal is understanding, not conquering.
- **Difference from other future books:** Quiet nighttime atmosphere and imagination rather than daytime action.

## Book 6 — Bailey & Omi and the Wildflower Detour

- **Core adventure / setting:** A spring walk on a dog-friendly foothill trail where bright wildflowers make the familiar route look completely new.
- **Bailey’s role:** Follows every interesting color and scent until she loses track of which turn they took.
- **Omi’s role:** Tries to rely on memory, then realizes seasonal changes have hidden her usual landmarks.
- **Conflict:** The sisters must slow down and retrace a safe, supervised route without trampling flowers or leaving the trail.
- **Emotional lesson:** Paying attention is part of protecting the places we love.
- **Story possibilities:** Repeated “look but don’t leap” moments, a trail marker hidden behind blooms, and a final viewpoint earned through patient observation.
- **Visual opportunities:** Luminous watercolor color fields, narrow paths through gold and purple blooms, close-up noses and paws that remain clearly on trail.
- **Distinctive promise:** Nature stewardship and seasonal change, rather than fear, competition, or weather.

## Book 7 — Bailey & Omi’s Harbor Surprise

- **Core adventure / setting:** A calm morning along a dog-friendly harbor promenade with boats, gulls, ramps, and unfamiliar mechanical sounds.
- **Bailey’s role:** Is fascinated by reflections and keeps searching for the “other dog” moving below the dock.
- **Omi’s role:** Understands the water but becomes uneasy when a dock shifts gently underfoot.
- **Conflict:** Each sister is comfortable with a different part of the harbor, so neither can lead the whole outing alone.
- **Emotional lesson:** Asking for help is another kind of confidence.
- **Story possibilities:** A bobbing reflection, a clanking halyard mystery, careful practice on a stable viewing platform, and a quiet shared boat-watching finish.
- **Visual opportunities:** Turquoise reflections, watercolor boats, long morning shadows, ripples that echo the dogs’ shapes.
- **Distinctive promise:** A working waterfront full of sound and motion, without turning the dogs into sailors or inventing unsafe access.

## Book 8 — Bailey & Omi and the Mountain Snow Day

- **Core adventure / setting:** A supervised day trip to a dog-friendly mountain snow-play area after a Southern California storm.
- **Bailey’s role:** Charges into soft snow and discovers that enthusiasm does not make short legs taller.
- **Omi’s role:** Moves carefully at first, then learns to play once Bailey shows her the soft, safe patches.
- **Conflict:** They must adapt their usual walking styles to slippery ground, cold paws, and a landscape with fewer familiar scents.
- **Emotional lesson:** New conditions call for new choices, not giving up.
- **Story possibilities:** Bailey’s disappearing paw prints, Omi testing each step, a shared path packed down together, and a warm-car ending.
- **Visual opportunities:** White paper used as snow, jewel-toned scarves or harnesses, visible breath, expressive tracks, warm/cool watercolor contrast.
- **Distinctive promise:** Cold-weather problem solving and physical adaptation, clearly separate from the water and nighttime stories.

## Book 9 — Bailey & Omi’s Farmers Market Mix-Up

- **Core adventure / setting:** A leashed visit to a dog-friendly outdoor farmers market during a quiet hour, full of approved smells, baskets, music, and neighbors.
- **Bailey’s role:** Notices that a dropped reusable shopping list has stuck to the outside of a rolling basket.
- **Omi’s role:** Wants to greet everyone and has to practice giving busy people and other dogs space.
- **Conflict:** The sisters try to help reunite the list with its owner while staying calm in a crowded, distracting place.
- **Emotional lesson:** Being considerate helps everyone belong in shared spaces.
- **Story possibilities:** Scent-based false leads, a repeating market musician, polite waiting, and a reunion near the final produce stall.
- **Visual opportunities:** Colorful produce shapes, striped awnings, varied people and dogs at respectful distances, lively but readable crowd scenes.
- **Distinctive promise:** Community manners and observation in a public setting, not wilderness navigation.

## Book 10 — Bailey & Omi and the Desert Footprints

- **Core adventure / setting:** An early-morning, cool-season walk on a dog-friendly desert nature trail with their grown-up carrying water and following posted rules.
- **Bailey’s role:** Becomes absorbed in tiny tracks crossing the sand and imagines increasingly enormous owners for them.
- **Omi’s role:** Knows some scents but must admit that not every mystery should be followed.
- **Conflict:** Curiosity pulls them toward the edge of the marked trail, where they choose observation from a safe distance instead.
- **Emotional lesson:** Respect sometimes means leaving a mystery undisturbed.
- **Story possibilities:** Tracks that overlap, wind erasing clues, a harmless animal glimpsed far away, and the dogs making their own paired trail back.
- **Visual opportunities:** Rose-and-gold desert washes, long shadows, delicate track patterns, big sky, restrained distant wildlife.
- **Distinctive promise:** Wildlife respect and unanswered curiosity, avoiding fabricated close encounters.

## Book 11 — Bailey & Omi’s Long Way Home

- **Core adventure / setting:** A neighborhood walk is rerouted by ordinary construction, sending the family through a pocket park, a pedestrian bridge, and streets they have never explored.
- **Bailey’s role:** Treats every turn as a discovery and helps keep the mood playful.
- **Omi’s role:** Misses the predictable route and worries they are going the wrong way.
- **Conflict:** The detour takes longer than expected, and both dogs must balance curiosity with the comfort of familiar routines.
- **Emotional lesson:** Home can still feel close when trusted companions are together.
- **Story possibilities:** Recognizing a familiar hill from a new angle, checking safe crossings, greeting a known neighbor in an unexpected place, and arriving home proudly tired.
- **Visual opportunities:** A visual route motif, changing neighborhood architecture, bridge views, the warm glow of the familiar front door.
- **Distinctive promise:** An emotionally grounded everyday adventure about flexibility, not a lost-dog story.

## Series-wide illustration and continuity guardrails

- Preserve the approved watercolor language, established Bailey and Omi designs, relative scale, markings, and recognizable expressions.
- Ground every outing in a real, dog-appropriate setting with a supervising grown-up and truthful safety details.
- Keep Bailey curious and observant and Omi experienced but imperfect; neither sister is always right.
- Vary the emotional problem and visual palette from book to book so the series feels connected without becoming repetitive.
- Treat these as concept candidates until a real memory/location, safety details, and illustration feasibility are confirmed.

## Book 2 green-light questions

Before outlining, confirm a real Bailey/Omi windy-day memory or location, choose only dog-safe objects for the chase, and decide whether the repeated comic beat is a leaf, napkin, or lightweight map. That is the next creative checkpoint; full writing and final illustration production remain intentionally unstarted.
