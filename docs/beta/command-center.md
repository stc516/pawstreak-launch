# PawStreak Beta Command Center

Purpose: get 10–25 real dog parents using PawStreak repeatedly without founder hand-holding.

Core promise: help dog parents give their dogs better days.

Primary loop: Discover → choose → go → remember → progress → discover again.

## Beta target

Start with 10–25 people who are likely to actually use PawStreak this week.

Best-fit testers:

- Dog parents in San Diego / Orange County.
- People who already take dog photos.
- People who walk their dog 3+ times per week.
- People who will text honest feedback quickly.
- People with iPhones first, then Android after iPhone flow is clean.

Avoid for first beta:

- People who only want pet sitting, training, or Rover-like services.
- People outside the first supported markets unless they understand local discovery may be light.
- People who will not send screenshots.

## Invite order

1. Founder/family devices.
2. 3–5 very friendly dog people.
3. 5–10 San Diego dog people.
4. 5–10 Orange County dog people.
5. Broader IG beta invite only after no P0/P1 issues for 24 hours.

## Beta link

Production site: https://pawstreakapp.com

Primary app entry: https://pawstreakapp.com/app

## What testers should do

Ask each tester to do one simple loop:

1. Create an account.
2. Add their dog.
3. Pick an adventure or Quick Walk.
4. Finish it.
5. Save a memory/photo.
6. Share or screenshot the result.
7. Come back for a second adventure within 7 days.

## What counts as a blocker

P0 — stop inviting people:

- Cannot sign up or sign in.
- Dog/profile does not persist.
- Adventure cannot finish.
- Memory/photo is lost after reload/sign-in.
- Private account data leaks across users.
- Account deletion fails.
- App is unusable on current iPhone Safari.

P1 — fix before expanding beta:

- Instagram image share does not attach the card.
- Push notification subscription fails silently.
- User does not understand what to do next.
- Major mobile layout overlap or clipped primary CTA.
- Journey feels empty/confusing after first memory.
- Fake/demo data appears in production app mode.

P2 — note but do not stop beta:

- Nice-to-have animation/polish.
- More trail content.
- Route recording.
- Calendar integration depth.
- More challenges/rewards.

## Daily founder rhythm

Morning, 10 minutes:

- Check new signups.
- Check bug reports.
- Check whether anyone completed an adventure.
- DM one tester who got stuck.

Evening, 10 minutes:

- Ask: did anyone save a memory today?
- Collect screenshots.
- Tag bugs P0/P1/P2.
- Decide whether tomorrow is invite-more or fix-first.

## Beta success targets

Minimum signal for beta to continue:

- 10 real signups.
- 7 complete onboarding.
- 5 start an adventure or Quick Walk.
- 3 save a memory.
- 2 come back for a second adventure.
- At least 1 person shares or asks to share.

Strong signal:

- 25 real signups.
- 18 complete onboarding.
- 12 start an adventure.
- 8 save a memory.
- 5 return for a second adventure.
- 3+ people say PawStreak made them do something better/different with their dog.

## Founder check-in questions

Send after first use:

1. Did you know what to do next?
2. Did anything break or feel confusing?
3. Did PawStreak make you want to do something better with your dog?
4. Would you use it again tomorrow or this weekend?
5. Send me a screenshot of the weirdest/best part.

## Freeze rule

Do not add new features during beta unless they fix a P0/P1 blocker.

Default decision rule:

Problem → evidence → proposed change → expected outcome → measurement → priority.
