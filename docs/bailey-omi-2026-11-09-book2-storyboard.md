## Characters and scene locked before image production
- **Bailey:** black-and-white female Husky puppy, small/fluffy, bright blue eyes, green collar, exact approved blaze/fur markings, curled tail. She notices the grass pattern and helps Omi.
- **Omi:** female, slightly larger golden-tan mixed rescue, smooth coat, cream muzzle/chest, pointed ears, brown eyes, pink collar, curved tail. Patient and dryly funny, surprised by unpredictable leaves. She is never reduced to a foolish sidekick.
- **Maggie:** preserve approved young Asian woman's face/hair/scale; proposed one-book outfit: blue sweater, jeans, walking shoes, matching established Book 1 reference. Change once in continuity sheet only, not between spreads.
- **Look:** watercolor/colored pencil, warm cream light with believable greens and sky blue, visible paper grain, credible paws and anatomy; copy lives in separate editable text layers.
- **Safety:** two attached leashes held by Maggie, no chasing flying objects, no off-leash dogs or hazardous shoreline. Dogs' dialogue is fictional; no claim Maggie understands dog speech.

## Single fictional park master plate (NOT a verified named destination)
Fixed camera looks across a level dog-permitted grassy park. A low wind-shaped shrub is in distant **left** background, a group of trees in distant **right** background, clear lawn in center. Maggie is seated by the near-right edge of a rectangular picnic cloth; Bailey is generally foreground-left and Omi right. Sunlight direction is consistently upper-left. No road, cliff, dock, crowds, food, signage, ocean access, or invented geography. All ten views must remain reconcilable to this layout.

**Prop continuity:** one cloth; the same near-right cloth corner comes loose at Spread 3 and is caught at Spread 7. Two leashes remain attached. Maggie holds leash handles in her **left** hand so her **right** hand can press or catch cloth. Leaves and bending grass show gusts; gust direction may change, so avoid a falsely fixed wind arrow. Do **not** silently introduce a bag, stake or weight as a “fastener.”

## Concept-to-manuscript mismatch (release gate)
The earlier Rank 1 slate described **scattered items and Omi running/retrieving them**, whereas manuscript v0.1 has **no retrieval and no off-leash chase**. Use v0.1 for the storyboard, not that obsolete action. “Windy-Day Chase” remains a **working title** that may need retitling if the quiet observational story survives the read-aloud test. Do not solve this by introducing unsafe dog chasing or secretly changing approved character behavior.

## Resolve before a single paid/new illustration
1. Spread 7 text says Maggie “fastened” the cloth, but contains no fastener prop. **Revise the manuscript to a physically shown action** (such as her holding it down) or explicitly introduce a consistent prop first.
2. Make corner movement Spread 3→7 and Maddie's free hand spatially unambiguous. (Maggie, not “Maddie,” in the final manuscript.)
3. Spread 9's paw-to-ear attempt must pass anatomical review; don't give Omi humanlike forelimbs.
4. Determine actual 24- vs 32-page imposition: 10 illustrated spreads equal 20 story pages, leaving front/back matter to allocate; check printer and ebook specs, don't add filler.
5. Rehearse aloud for ages 3–7 and record observed comprehension before changing copy; don't invent reading-test results.
