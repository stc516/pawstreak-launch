## Full description

PawStreak helps dog parents give their dogs better days.

Discover dog-friendly places near you, choose an adventure, get out the door, and save the memory when you are done. PawStreak is built for the daily dog-parent loop: find something worth doing, go do it, remember it, and keep discovering more.

Use PawStreak to:

- Find dog-friendly adventures nearby
- Start a quick walk when you just need to get moving
- Save photos and notes from the day
- Build a Journey of the places you and your dog have explored
- Get optional reminders so better dog days do not slip away
- Share clean adventure cards with friends or Instagram

PawStreak is not a hardcore GPS tracker or a pet-care dashboard. It is a simple way to make your dog’s life feel a little bigger than the same old walk.

Your dog deserves more than the same old walk.

## TestFlight beta description

PawStreak is a beta app for dog parents who want to give their dogs better days.

Please test the core loop:

1. Create an account
2. Add your dog
3. Explore dog-friendly places
4. Start a Quick Walk or adventure
5. Add a photo
6. Finish and save the memory
7. Share a card if you want
8. Return later and find another adventure

Report anything confusing, broken, slow, or emotionally flat. The goal is a stable app that real dog owners can use without founder help.
