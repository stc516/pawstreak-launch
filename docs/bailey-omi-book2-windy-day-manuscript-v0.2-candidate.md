# Bailey & Omi and the Windy-Day Surprise
**Bailey & Omi Adventures · Book 2 candidate · manuscript v0.2 · November 10, 2026**  
**Author:** S.T. Carroll  
**Status:** Unapproved story development. Alternate working title to *Bailey & Omi and the Windy-Day Chase* (v0.1). No canon change, commissioned art, published book, claimed real incident, or release date.

## Story text — ten proposed illustrated spreads

### Spread 1
At the park, the grass bent one way, then the other.

Bailey sniffed the ground. So many new smells! Which one should a little Husky investigate first?

Omi lifted her nose. “I know this kind of day,” she said. “A day to keep an eye on everything.”

### Spread 2
Maggie spread a picnic cloth on the grass. The sisters settled beside her, their leashes safely in her hand.

*Whoooosh!*

Bailey's fluffy tail puffed up.

Omi's tall ears flattened sideways.

“You look like a flying pancake,” Bailey said.

“I was about to say the same,” Omi replied.

### Spread 3
One corner of the cloth flapped up.

“It's getting away!” cried Omi.

She sprang to her feet—then stopped right beside Maggie.

Maggie pressed the loose corner against the grass with her free hand.

Omi watched very carefully. Surely *she* could tell where the wind would go next.

### Spread 4
A dry leaf skittered past.

“To the left,” Omi announced.

The leaf went right.

“Ah,” said Omi. “A surprising leaf.”

Another leaf spun around her paws and landed behind her.

“Two surprising leaves.”

### Spread 5
Bailey wasn't watching the leaves anymore. She was watching the grass.

It leaned. It straightened. It swirled in a little circle.

*Whoooosh went the wind. Which way would it go?*

She crouched low and followed the bending blades with her bright blue eyes.

### Spread 6
“Come see,” she told Omi.

“There's nothing there,” Omi said.

“Not yet.”

Omi looked at the empty patch. Waiting was not much of a plan.

Still, she stepped close enough that their shoulders almost touched.

### Spread 7
The grass leaned. A leaf looped back. The loose cloth corner lifted again—right toward Maggie's waiting hand.

“There!” barked Omi.

Maggie caught the corner and held it flat until the gust passed.

Bailey gave a pleased little wiggle.

Omi looked from the grass to Bailey.

“You were watching all along.”

“I was *trying*,” Bailey said.

### Spread 8
The cloth was still. The wind, however, had one more idea.

*WHOOOOOSH!*

Bailey's fur became a cloud. Omi's ears folded in two different directions.

For a moment they stared at each other.

Then Bailey gave one delighted bark, and even Omi couldn't keep a straight face.

### Spread 9
Omi shook her head. One ear sprang up. The other stayed folded.

“A very determined breeze,” she said.

Bailey settled beside her sister. Together they watched one last leaf twirl into a quiet patch of grass.

Neither tried to guess where it would land.

### Spread 10
When the wind finally eased, Maggie folded the picnic cloth. The sisters waited while she gathered their things.

“Next time,” Omi said, “I might watch the grass first.”

“And I'll ask what the sky smells like,” Bailey replied.

The breeze brushed their noses as they walked away, leashes safely in Maggie's hand.

This time, both sisters looked up.

## Change log from v0.1 (editorial proposal, not approved)
1. **Title honesty:** *Windy-Day Surprise* reflects the leaf-and-grass observational story; *Chase* misleadingly suggests an off-leash pursuit or retrieving picnic debris. The existing ranked slate and v0.1 remain unchanged until the author chooses a title.
2. **Spread 7 causality and continuity:** Maggie catches and **holds** the same cloth corner rather than “fastening” it with an unintroduced fastener. Spread 10 folds the cloth only after wind eases. Illustration must show her left hand holding both leash handles and right hand on the near-right cloth corner.
3. **Spread 9 dog anatomy:** Omi performs a plausible head shake, not a humanlike paw-to-ear movement. No extra props, imaginary wildlife, food, or invented geographical features.
4. **Sisterhood:** Bailey's attention and Omi's willingness to wait form the emotional payoff; Omi is wry, capable, and encouraging rather than an incompetent sidekick.
5. **No false biography:** The park and speech are invented. Real Bailey and Meiomi inspired the characters; this particular outing is **not** documented as a true event.

## Ten-spread illustration continuity gate
- First show low shrub distant left and trees distant right in a **fictional unnamed level, dog-permitted park**. Maggie seated to the right of the rectangular picnic cloth; Bailey foreground left and Omi just right. Never invent a named real destination. Keep sunlight upper-left and location axis consistent.
- Two collars and leashes visible and attached throughout: Bailey green collar, small black-and-white female Husky puppy, blue eyes and established blaze; Omi pink collar, larger golden-tan mixed rescue, pointed ears. Maggie matches the **approved** young Asian woman in Book 1. Keep restrained watercolor/colored-pencil warmth and realistic anatomy.
- The **same near-right cloth corner** flaps in spreads 3 and 7; Maggie's right hand presses, catches and holds it. A second gust in spread 8 can affect ears/fur while her hand stays on the cloth.
- No lettering inside flattened artwork; plan editable type and safe areas. **Ten spreads = twenty story pages**; verify 24- versus 32-page production pagination before art begins.
- Do not generate or commission new book illustrations until title, manuscript, visual model sheets and safety staging have been approved.
