# Bailey & Omi and the Windy-Day Chase

**Bailey & Omi Adventures · Book 2 candidate · manuscript v0.1 · 8 November 2026**  
**Author credit:** S.T. Carroll  
**Status:** Original, unapproved fictional draft. No publication, ISBN, cover, commissioned art, promised release, or asserted real-life incident.

## Story text — 10 proposed read-aloud spreads

### Spread 1
At the park, the grass bent one way, then the other.

Bailey sniffed the ground. There were so many new smells that she hardly knew where to begin.

Omi lifted her nose. “I know this kind of day,” she said. “A day for keeping an eye on everything.”

### Spread 2
Maggie spread a picnic cloth on the grass. Both dogs settled beside her, their leashes held safely in her hand.

*Whoooosh!*

Bailey's fluffy tail puffed up.

Omi's tall ears flattened sideways.

Bailey blinked. “You look like a flying pancake.”

“I was about to say the same,” said Omi.

### Spread 3
One corner of the cloth flapped up.

“It's getting away!” cried Omi.

She sprang to her feet—then stopped beside Maggie.

Maggie pressed down the cloth with one hand.

Omi kept her eyes on the loose corner. Surely she could tell where it would go next.

### Spread 4
A dry leaf skittered past.

“To the left,” Omi announced.

The leaf went right.

“Ah,” said Omi. “A surprising leaf.”

Another leaf spun around her paws and landed behind her.

“Two surprising leaves.”

### Spread 5
Bailey didn't watch the leaves. She watched the grass.

It leaned. It straightened. It swirled in a little circle.

*Whoooosh went the wind. Which way would it go?*

Bailey put her nose close to the ground and followed the bending blades with her eyes.

### Spread 6
“Over here,” she told Omi.

“There's nothing over there,” Omi replied.

“Not yet.”

Omi looked at the empty patch of grass. Waiting was not much of a plan.

Still, she shuffled closer until their shoulders almost touched.

### Spread 7
The grass leaned again.

A leaf looped back.

And the loose corner of the cloth fluttered right toward Maggie's hand.

“There!” barked Omi.

Maggie caught the corner and fastened it down.

Bailey gave a pleased little wiggle. Omi looked from the cloth to the grass, and then to Bailey.

“You saw that coming.”

“Sort of,” Bailey said. “I watched it come.”

### Spread 8
The picnic was safe. The wind, however, had one more idea.

*WHOOOOOSH!*

Bailey's fur stood out like a cloud. Omi's ears folded in two different directions.

For a moment, both sisters stared at each other.

Then Bailey burst into a happy bark.

### Spread 9
Omi tried to smooth an ear with her paw. It sprang right back up.

“A very determined breeze,” she said.

Bailey settled against her sister. The two of them watched one last leaf twirl into a soft patch of grass.

Neither dog tried to guess its next move.

### Spread 10
Maggie packed away the picnic cloth. The sisters waited together while she gathered their things.

“Next time,” Omi said, “I might watch the grass first.”

“Next time,” Bailey said, “you can tell me what the sky smells like.”

The wind swept over their noses as they walked away together.

This time, they both looked up.
