1. **P0 — Today's actual adventure versus stale "Today" text.** Source proves new journey entries persist `date:'Today'` **and** a timestamp. Home previously chose any record with that text, even from an earlier day. This branch resolves by local calendar day and preserves the seeded demo. Pure regression check attached. **Code fix, not a visually retested release.**
2. **P0 — Active adventure + pack consistency.** The Home resume and per-dog Journey/recap work is already in separate draft PRs [#80](https://github.com/stc516/pawstreak-launch/pull/80), [#76](https://github.com/stc516/pawstreak-launch/pull/76), [#79](https://github.com/stc516/pawstreak-launch/pull/79), [#82](https://github.com/stc516/pawstreak-launch/pull/82). 3. **P1 — Discovery reliability.** The Explore screenshot displays **"Map unavailable right now"** with a truthful clickable place list. The deterministic release gate sets `VITE_MAPBOX_TOKEN=''`, so this **does not prove a live production Mapbox outage**. Verify real token, GPS permission/denial, marker-selection behavior and native SDK device performance.
4. **P1 — Real-photo versus decorative art distinction.** The adventure-ready screenshot has a large, intentionally photo-free `NO PHOTO ADDED` block. Keep the honesty gate `authenticImageUrl` (no stock/fake venue photo); improve typography/illustrated *category* identity only after visual review, without implying a verified location photo. The completion and Journey share screenshots properly show zero photos where none exists.
5. **P1 — Emotional memories and completion layout.** Review iPhone short-height scroll and sticky `Save this adventure` CTA against the `06-save-memory-payoff.png` capture, including 200% text enlargement and reduced motion. Verify photo-optional completion, share card and two-dog record continuity.
6. **P2 — Accessibility and hierarchy.** The onboarding account entry is legible in `02-app-entry.png`, but remaining six-step onboarding, Challenges, Achievements and Rewards were source-reviewed **without current screenshots**. Audit keyboard/screen-reader states, tap areas, contrast and category illustration consistency before asserting polish.
7. **P2 — Controlled retention.** Continue meaningful weekly challenges, saved memories and next outings; avoid streak pressure, fictional activity counters or auto-scrolling loops.
