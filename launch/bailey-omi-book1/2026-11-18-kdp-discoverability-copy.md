## Copy/paste retailer description — plain text
Bailey has never climbed the stairs. To a small Husky puppy, they look impossibly high.

Maggie is cheering from above, and Omi—Bailey's patient older sister—is close by. Bailey takes a breath, tries one step, and slips. She could stop right there. Or she could take her time and try again.

*Bailey's Big Climb*, the first Bailey & Omi Adventures picture book by S.T. Carroll, is a gentle read-aloud for ages 3–7 about facing something unfamiliar, getting encouragement without being rushed, and discovering that a small step can be a brave one.

Inspired by the personalities of real dogs Bailey and Meiomi, this illustrated story gives families a simple conversation starter: What helps us feel ready to try?
