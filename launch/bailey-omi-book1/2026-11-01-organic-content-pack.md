## Reusable family activity — “One Small Brave Step” (copy for parent handout)
**Read together:** Ask “What did Bailey notice before she decided what to do?” and “How did Omi help by staying nearby?”  
**Try together:** Pick a safe, dog-friendly change of scenery. Let the dog sniff, watch, or pause. Adults supervise children, observe signs of comfort, follow leash rules, and never force a fearful dog.  
**Remember together:** Draw or describe one new thing you noticed. Ask “What helped you feel comfortable?”  
**Not a competition:** You don't need stairs, a long walk, or a photograph to complete this activity.
