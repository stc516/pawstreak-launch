### Real Bailey & Omi introduction

The dogs in the book are real. Bailey is the blue-eyed husky: curious, energetic, and sometimes unsure about unfamiliar things. Omi is her older rescue sister: observant, confident, and always ready to join the adventure. Their relationship is the heart of *Bailey & Omi Adventures*.

### The stairs / inspiration story

Before there was a book, there was a little husky looking up at something that felt enormous. Bailey’s unsure moments—and Omi’s calm company—became the spark for **Bailey’s Big Climb**. It isn’t really a story about stairs. It’s about the way a small step can become a big one.

### Behind the scenes

Bringing Bailey and Omi to the page meant protecting what makes them recognizable: Bailey’s black-and-white fluff, blue eyes, and green collar; Omi’s golden-tan coat, pointed ears, and pink collar. The finished world uses warm watercolor and colored-pencil texture so it feels as tactile and adventurous as their real days together.

### Meet Bailey

Meet Bailey: a small Siberian Husky with bright blue eyes, a green collar, and a very large supply of curiosity. She doesn’t begin every adventure feeling brave—but she keeps noticing what might be worth trying.

### Meet Omi

Meet Omi, also known as Meiomi: a golden-tan rescue from the streets of Mexico with unmistakable ears and older-sister confidence. Sometimes she shows Bailey the way. Sometimes Bailey surprises her. The best adventures are the ones they figure out together.

### Series introduction

Two real dogs. A world of little discoveries. *Bailey & Omi Adventures* is a picture-book series about curiosity, courage, friendship, and the funny things that happen when two sisters meet something new.
