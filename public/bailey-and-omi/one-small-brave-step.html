One Small Brave Step

A little courage can start with noticing, pausing, and choosing what feels right.

Inspired by Bailey’s Big Climb, the first Bailey & Omi Adventures picture book by S.T. Carroll. You don’t need stairs or a dog to join in.

Explore the book
Three gentle steps

Notice. Pause. Try—or choose again.

01 / NOTICE

What feels new?

It might be a place, sound, question, or something you want to learn. What do you notice about it?

02 / PAUSE

What would help?

Ask someone to stay nearby, watch for a while, or take a break. It is okay to change your mind.

03 / CHOOSE

What next?

Try one little step if it feels right—or wait until another day. Either choice can be thoughtful.

After reading together

Three questions to talk about

What made the staircase seem so big to Bailey?

How did having Omi nearby help her feel supported?

What is a small brave step you have taken—or might take someday?

For grown-ups

Real dogs get to choose, too.

If you include your dog in a real-life activity, keep it safe, supervised, and voluntary. Follow leash rules; let your dog sniff, pause, or move away; and never force stairs or unfamiliar situations. Children should always have adult guidance.
