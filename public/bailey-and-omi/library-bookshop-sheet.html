The story

Bailey has never climbed the stairs. They look impossibly high. While Maggie cheers from the top, her older rescue sister Omi waits nearby. Bailey tries, slips, and faces a decision: stop for now or attempt one more little step.

Inspired by the real Bailey and Omi, this story gives young readers room to talk about feeling unsure, asking for support, and moving at their own pace.

Why it works for storytime

Short, concrete visual conflict; recognizable dog personalities; a gentle emotional turn; and questions children can answer in their own words.

A 15-minute read-aloud conversation

  1. Before reading: What might make a staircase look very big?
  2. After reading: What did Bailey notice? How did Omi help by staying close?
  3. Creative response: Draw a small new thing you'd like to notice, try, or learn—with someone you trust nearby.

Real-life dog activities should be voluntary and grown-up supervised. Never force a dog onto stairs or into unfamiliar situations.
