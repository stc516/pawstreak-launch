The dogs behind the stories

Meet the real Bailey & Omi

These characters began with two very real dogs. Their everyday discoveries, unsure moments, and adventures together are the heart of this series.

Bailey

A small, fluffy female Siberian Husky with black-and-white fur, bright blue eyes, and a green collar. The younger sister: curious, energetic, and sometimes a little unsure of something new.

Omi / Meiomi

An older golden-tan mixed-breed rescue from the streets of Mexico, with big pointed ears and a pink collar. Slightly larger than Bailey, she brings confidence and an older sister’s watchful company.

Exploration. Confidence. Connection. Memories. Sometimes Omi helps Bailey. Sometimes Bailey surprises Omi. Sometimes they figure it out together.
