Try Bailey’s Big Climb

You don’t need stairs. Your family’s “big climb” can be any small, safe new experience that lets your dog explore at their own pace.

  1. Choose a little something new

    Visit somewhere new, take a different walking route, or explore a dog-friendly park or trail. You could also gently practice something your dog is unsure about, starting from a comfortable distance.

  2. Let your dog set the pace

    Keep it short and relaxed. Let them sniff, pause, or move away. Encourage curiosity; never pull, force, or frighten them. If they seem uncomfortable, give them space and try something easier another day.

  3. Keep the little memory

    Take a photo when everyone is comfortable. Talk together about what your dog explored and what helped them feel at ease. A tiny discovery still counts.

A grown-up leads the adventure

Choose somewhere safe and suitable for your dog, follow local leash rules, and supervise children. Skip stairs or any activity that isn’t right for your dog. There’s no finish line to reach and no photo worth making them uncomfortable.
